03

Make your film

Lossless master preserves native video pixels, bit depth and frames without resizing or fixed-frame-rate conversion. Video uses lossless FFV1; audio uses uncompressed PCM. Trim, speed and audio edits still change the content. Clips need matching dimensions, orientation, pixel format and color settings; audio needs matching sample rates and channels. Large MKV files are for keeping a master, not direct Instagram uploads; play them in a compatible player such as VLC.

Place and trim music on the audio tracks. Looping is optional in each audio clip’s controls. Set video volume to 0% to use music alone. HDR footage is rejected until color-managed export is available, to avoid silently changing its colors.
