Sharing from your phone

After installing the sharing bridge in Termux, choose Share → Termux in YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or X/Twitter. Media Hub opens with the link ready for you to review.

You can also install Media Hub from your browser menu. On supported browsers, the installed app can appear directly in the Share menu. If it does not, use Share → Termux or Copy link.

Termux and the Media Hub service need to be running. Private posts, Stories and login-required videos may not be available.
